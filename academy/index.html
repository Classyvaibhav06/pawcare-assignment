FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to know coding?

No. The Creator & Ops tracks are fully no-code. The Builder track teaches just enough JS/Python to glue APIs together.

What if I miss a live session?

Every session is recorded and posted within 2 hours, with lab instructions you can do on your own time.

Will I need paid AI subscriptions?

No. Everything is designed to run on free tiers; we flag where a paid plan genuinely helps.

Is there placement support?

We don't promise placements. We do share internship/job leads in the alumni group and review your portfolio and resume.
